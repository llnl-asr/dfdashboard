Shortcuts
=========

The timeline answers to the keys and the mouse gestures below.
The same table is in the app. Press ``?`` to open it.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 34 66

   * - Input
     - Effect
   * - drag or two-finger swipe
     - Pan through time.
   * - wheel
     - Scroll the lanes.
   * - ctrl+wheel or pinch
     - Zoom in or out at the cursor.
   * - ``W`` or up arrow
     - Zoom in.
   * - ``S`` or down arrow
     - Zoom out.
   * - ``A`` or left arrow
     - Pan left.
   * - ``D`` or right arrow
     - Pan right.
   * - double-click
     - Zoom in on the point.
   * - drag on the ruler or shift+drag
     - Measure a range and show its stats.
   * - click a slice
     - Show the details of that event.
   * - ``Ctrl+F``
     - Focus the search box.
   * - ``Enter`` or ``Shift+Enter``
     - Jump to the next or the previous match.
   * - click or drag the minimap
     - Jump to a region or zoom into it.
   * - ``?``
     - Show or hide this list.
   * - ``Escape``
     - Close the list or clear the selection.

The arrow keys do the same as ``W``, ``A``, ``S`` and ``D``.
No modifier is needed, so the timeline takes the keys when no text field has the focus.
